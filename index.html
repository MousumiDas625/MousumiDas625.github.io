MD

I am a researcher focused on robot learning — the intersection of imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and robotic manipulation. My work asks how robots can learn efficiently and reliably: by exploiting the structure in expert demonstrations, by dynamically composing policies at execution time, and by grounding language and perception in real-world action.

I am currently a Research Intern at the Georgia Tech Research Institute (GTRI) with Colin Usher, building vision-based pick-selection systems for deformable objects. At USC, I work in the LIRA Lab with Erdem Bıyık on TV-BC, a teaching-volume–weighted imitation learning method, and in the GLAMOR Lab with Jesse Thomason on SWAP, a stepwise policy-routing framework for vision-language-action models deployed on the Franka Panda arm.

I am pursuing my M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering (Machine Learning & Data Science) at the University of Southern California (Jan 2025 – Dec 2026).

Foundation-Model Pick Selection for Deformable Objects with Human-in-the-Loop Ranking

Mousumi Das, Colin Usher

We design a vision-based pick-selection system for deformable objects in high-throughput settings that decouples perception from selection using three frozen foundation models — SAM2 for instance segmentation, DINOv2 for visual embeddings augmented with geometric depth features, and AnyGrasp for 6-DOF grasp generation. The only learned component is a lightweight pairwise ranking MLP trained on VR operator demonstrations with a RankNet objective, and a human-in-the-loop adaptation loop lets operator corrections continuously improve the ranker without scheduled retraining.

In preparation. Target: ICRA 2027.

Imitation Learning from Deliberative Teachers

Mousumi Das, Erdem Bıyık

Human demonstrators don't pick demonstrations at random — they teach. TV-BC (Teaching-Volume Behavioral Cloning) exploits this by upweighting demonstrations where the learner's loss exceeds the expert's, prioritizing hard, informative examples via a softmax-weighted BC loss derived from machine teaching theory. On Procgen CoinRun, TV-BC improves early-round solve rate by 21.7%, out-of-distribution performance by 16.7% at peak, and reduces cross-seed variance by 4× over standard behavioral cloning.

In preparation. Target: ICLR 2027.

SWAP: Stepwise Action Policy Routing for Vision-Language-Action Models

Mousumi Das, Aditeya Prajapati, Abrar Anwar, Jesse Thomason

No single robot policy is best at every step of a manipulation task. SWAP dynamically composes multiple VLA and control policies during execution: we formulate policy routing as an offline reinforcement learning problem and train a routing critic that selects among π0, OpenVLA, and keypoint-based controllers at each decision step. Evaluated on real-world DROID manipulation tasks and LIBERO simulation, SWAP improves task success by up to 33% while reducing successful-trajectory length by 28.3% versus fixed-policy and routing baselines.

Submitted to CoRL 2026.

DiPS: Dialogue Policy Selection for High-Stakes Persuasion Agents

Tianyi Zhang*, Mousumi Das*, Abrar Anwar, Jesse Thomason, David Traum

LLMs struggle to persuade in high-stakes scenarios where one-size-fits-all strategies fail. In a wildfire-evacuation domain, DiPS learns to dynamically select persuasion strategies as the conversation evolves: an IQL-trained critic picks a persuasion policy at each turn from the resident's recent utterances, which then conditions a RAG-augmented LLM response. DiPS achieves higher evacuation success than zero-shot LLM and generic RAG baselines in both simulated and real human interactions.

SIGDIAL 2026 — ACL/ISCA Special Interest Group on Discourse and Dialogue.
