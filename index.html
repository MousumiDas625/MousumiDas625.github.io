01 About

I am a researcher focused on robot learning — the intersection of reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and robotic manipulation. My work asks how robots can learn efficiently and reliably from humans: by treating demonstrations as pedagogical signals, by selecting the right policy at the right moment, and by grounding language and perception in real-world action.

Currently I am a Research Assistant in the LIRA Lab (Prof. Erdem Bıyık) and the GLAMOR Lab (Prof. Jesse Thomason) at USC, working on teacher-aware imitation learning and multi-policy evaluation for tabletop manipulation with real-world deployment on a Franka Panda arm.

I am applying to PhD programs to deepen this research on general, sample-efficient, and human-aligned robot autonomy.

02 Research Interests

🦾

Imitation & Teacher-Aware Learning

Modeling human demonstrations as pedagogically selected rather than reward-maximizing — a Teaching-Volume–based behavioral cloning framework for better sample efficiency.

🎯

Reinforcement Learning

Policy learning and offline RL (e.g. Implicit Q-Learning) for adaptive, real-time decision-making and policy selection in interactive settings.

🤖

Robot Manipulation

Tabletop manipulation and multi-policy evaluation across π0, OpenVLA, and keypoint controllers, with deployment on the Franka Panda arm.

🗣️

Language-Grounded Robotics

Grounding language in actions and multimodal observations — context-aware RAG, semantic retrieval, and instruction-tuned LLMs for adaptive interaction.
